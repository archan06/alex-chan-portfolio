# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers for software and AI engineering internships (primary). They skim fast, often on a laptop between other candidates, sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn or email link. Classmates and others are secondary.

## Product Purpose
Alex Chan's personal site at alexrchan.dev: show who Alex is, what he has built and where he has worked, so a recruiter opens the resume, tries a live project or emails him. Success is a recruiter reaching out.

## Positioning
A student who ships real, live, multi-user products (an AI fantasy football analyzer, an AI resume analyzer, a job application tracker) and has an AI engineering internship, with a people-facing background (coaching, hospital lab, retail, fraternity VP External).

## Operating Context
- Single static page on GitHub Pages (`index.html`, `CNAME` = alexrchan.dev), plus `Alex-Chan-Resume.pdf` and `headshot.jpg`.
- No build step; libraries load from CDNs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections and facts must all stay: hero, about (with stats), experience (6 roles), projects (Loki Analyzer, ResumeAI, Landed + "more on the way"), skills (4 groups), education (Stony Brook, B.S. Information Systems, Technology Systems Management concentration, expected May 2028, GPA 3.25, VP External Lambda Phi Epsilon, coursework), certifications, contact.
- Copy: keep Alex's own words (light trims to fit are fine; ask before rewriting).
- Must work on phones without horizontal scroll.

## Brand Commitments
- Color scheme is binding (confirmed 2026-10-21): warm near-black grounds (#0A0907, #110E0A, #161210), amber #F59E0B / #FCD34D as the main accent, rose #F43F5E / #FDA4AF as the second accent, warm cream text #F5F0E8 with muted #A89880.
- Must not look like a generic AI-generated developer portfolio (the reason a previous redesign was rejected).

## Evidence on Hand
Real projects with live demos (Loki Analyzer on Streamlit Cloud, ResumeAI on Render, Landed on Vercel) and public source for ResumeAI and Landed; resume PDF; headshot. No testimonials, metrics or employer quotes; never invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over adjectives: live demos and real details carry the page.
2. A recruiter finds projects, resume and contact within seconds.
3. Unmistakably Alex's, never a template.
